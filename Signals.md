# Traces

- Traces give us the picture of what happens when a req is made to an application. Whether your application is a monolith with a single database or sophisticated mesh of services, trace are essential to understanding the full "path" a request takes in your application.

# Metrics

- A metrics is a measurement of a service captured at runtime. The moment of captured a measurement is know as a metrics event. which consist not only of the measurement itself, but also the time at which it was captured and associated metadata.
